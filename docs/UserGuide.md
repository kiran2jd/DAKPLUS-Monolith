# 📖 DAKPlus: Simple User Guide

Welcome to DAKPlus! This guide will help you navigate the app and start your journey toward success in postal exams.

---

### Step 1: Login or Sign Up 👤
- Open the app and enter your details to register or log in.
- Your progress is saved automatically under your account.

### Step 2: Choose Your Course 🎯
- On the **Dashboard**, you will see different exam banners:
  - **MTS Exam**
  - **Postman / Mail Guard**
  - **PA / SA Exam**
  - **Combined Course** (Access to all exams!)
- **Tap a banner** to enter that specific exam room.

### Step 3: Explore Topics & Tests 📚
- Once inside a course, you can:
  - View the **Syllabus** to see what topics are covered.
  - Browse available **Mock Tests**.
- Tests labeled **[PRO]** require a one-time purchase to unlock.

### Step 4: Unlock Your Success 🔓
- If a test or course is locked, tap the **"Buy Now"** banner at the bottom or the test itself.
- Complete the payment securely. 
- **Important**: Once payment is successful, the app will automatically bring you back to your dashboard, and your course will be unlocked!

### Step 5: Take a Mock Test ✍️
- Tap **"Start Test"** on any unlocked exam.
- **Timer**: The clock starts as soon as you begin.
- **Focus**: Stay on the screen! If you try to exit, the app will ask for confirmation to prevent accidental loss of progress.
- **Submit**: Once finished, tap **"Submit"** to see your results.

### Step 6: Track Your Progress 📈
- Tap **"Performance Hub"** on the menu to see your real scores.
- Look at the **Weekly Progress** chart to see how much you've improved.
- Check **"My Purchases"** in the side menu to see all your transactions.

---

**Happy Studying! 🚀**
*For any support, visit the "Support Center" in the side menu.*
